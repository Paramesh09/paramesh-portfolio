About

I care about the part users don't see.

Interfaces are the visible 10% of a product. Underneath sits the part that decides whether it actually works: the API contracts, the database schema, the auth flow, the job that runs at 3am. I spend most of my time there — making sure it's correct, fast, and doesn't fall over when something upstream changes.

On the frontend, that same discipline shows up as fewer re-renders, predictable state, and interfaces that stay usable when the network is slow or the data is incomplete.
